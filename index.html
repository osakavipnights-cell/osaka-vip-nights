Questions

Frequently asked questions.

What is included in the guide fee?

The guide fee covers private planning and guide service. Food and beverages, transportation, entrance or facility fees, and personal expenses are separate.

How many guests can join?

Each standard price is for one group of up to three guests. For groups of four or more, ¥30,000 is added per additional guest.

Can you help with reservations and transportation?

Yes. Reservation support and transportation coordination can be arranged according to your plan. Vehicle costs depend on the selected vehicle and are quoted separately.

Which languages can you support?

We support international visitors in English, Japanese, Chinese and Korean. Tell us your needs when you enquire.

When are you available?

Enquiries and service coordination are available 24 hours a day, every day.
